# Q3 2026 sales summary (July to September)

Prepared by Claire Morgan, finance team, Acme Outdoor Gear.

- Revenue: NZD 1.84 million, up 6.2% on Q3 2025.
- Online share: 41% of revenue (Q2: 38%).
- Best-selling line: the Tasman 2-person tent (1,210 units).
- Weakest line: trail-running packs, down 14% after a late shipment.
- Gross margin: 47.5%, steady on Q2.

Stores: Ponsonby and Newmarket grew fastest; Christchurch was flat while the Riccarton site was refitted.

Next steps: reorder the Tasman tent before the summer peak and review pack pricing with the buying team.
